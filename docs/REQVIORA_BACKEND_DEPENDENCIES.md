## Frontend policy until dependencies land

- Do not persist access or refresh tokens in browser storage.
- Do not invent endpoints, retry/cancel buttons, ticket replies, refund completion or entitlement repair actions.
- Treat backend authorization as authoritative and surface supported conflict/rate-limit errors safely.
